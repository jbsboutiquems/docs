Getting your account and profile configured properly is worth a few minutes up front. Setting the right timezone keeps your schedule accurate, choosing your display name makes the app feel personal, and picking a focus mode means Life OS shows up in a way that suits how you actually want to work. This page walks you through all of it.

## Creating Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up with your email">
    Go to [app.offscript.life](https://app.offscript.life) and click **Get Started**. Enter your email address and a secure password, then click **Create Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email address">
    Check your inbox for a confirmation email from Life OS. Click the **Verify Email** link inside. If it doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend confirmation email** on the sign-up page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your display name">
    After verification, Life OS will prompt you to enter a display name. This is how the app addresses you — choose whatever feels right, whether that's your first name, a nickname, or something else entirely. You can change it any time from your profile settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Profile

Once your account is active, take a moment to round out your profile. Open the sidebar, click your name or avatar at the bottom, and select **Profile Settings**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Updating your name and avatar">
    Click **Edit Profile** to change your display name at any time. To add or update your avatar, click the profile image placeholder and upload a photo from your device — any square image works well. Your avatar appears throughout the app and in any shared views.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Setting your timezone">
    Your timezone is one of the most important settings in Life OS. It ensures that scheduled events, daily habit resets, and your Today view all align with your actual clock — not the server's.

    Under **Profile Settings**, find the **Timezone** dropdown and select your current timezone. If you travel frequently and want the app to follow you, enable **Auto-detect timezone** and Life OS will update it automatically based on your device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changing your email or password">
    To update your email address or password, go to **Profile Settings → Security**. Changing your email will trigger a re-verification step to confirm the new address. For password changes, you'll be asked to enter your current password first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Choosing Your Focus Mode

Life OS can adapt to where you are in building your system. When you first sign in, you'll be prompted to choose a **Focus Mode** — you can revisit this choice any time under **Settings → Focus Mode**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Calm Mode">
    **Calm Mode** strips the interface back to the essentials: Today, Habits, and a simple Goals list. Fewer panels, fewer prompts, less visual noise. This is a great starting point if you want to ease into the system without feeling overwhelmed, or if you simply prefer a minimal day-to-day experience.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Full Mode">
    **Full Mode** unlocks the complete feature set: Life Areas, Projects, the full Schedule (day, week, and month views), Reflect, and all advanced settings. Choose this if you're ready to build a complete personal system from day one, or once you've outgrown Calm Mode and want more structure.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Your privacy matters. All data you enter in Life OS is stored securely and encrypted in transit. Your personal information and life data are never sold to third parties, never used for advertising, and never shared without your explicit consent.
</Note>

<Tip>
  For the full picture on how Life OS handles your data — including export options and account deletion — visit the [Data & Privacy settings](/settings/data-privacy) page.
</Tip>
