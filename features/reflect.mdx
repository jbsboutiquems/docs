Living intentionally isn't just about planning — it's about learning from what actually happens. The Reflect space in Life OS closes the loop between your intentions and your reality. A short daily journal captures the texture of your day, and the weekly review gives you a structured moment to look back, acknowledge what moved, and set clear intentions for the week ahead. Without reflection, even the best plans stay disconnected from your growth. With it, every week becomes a little more yours.

## The Daily Journal

The daily journal is a low-friction way to record how your day actually went — not a productivity report, just an honest note to your future self.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open today's journal entry">
    Tap **Reflect** in the bottom navigation, then tap **Today's Entry**. A fresh page opens, timestamped and ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the optional prompt">
    At the top of the entry you'll see a single prompt: *What moved today?* It's optional — answer it if it helps you get started, skip it if you already know what you want to write. The prompt is designed to surface momentum, not force a format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write freely">
    Type as much or as little as you like. There's no word count, no scoring. Even two sentences is enough to build the habit of closing the day intentionally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the entry">
    Tap **Done** and the entry is saved to your journal. It's stored privately, tied to the date, and instantly searchable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All your journal entries are stored in chronological order and fully searchable by keyword. Tap the search icon in the Reflect header to find an entry by a word, a date, or the name of a project or goal you mentioned.

## The Weekly Review

The weekly review is a structured flow that takes you through the last seven days and sets you up for the next seven. It's the most important ritual in the Life OS loop — the moment where scattered data becomes genuine insight.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review last week's habits">
    The review opens with a summary of every habit that was active last week. See which ones you completed consistently, which slipped, and on which specific days. Don't judge — just notice. This data tells you where your system is working and where real life is pushing back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review goal progress">
    Next, step through each active Goal. How far did you move on the next step? Did anything shift in priority? You can update step progress directly from this screen without leaving the review flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note wins and blockers">
    Write a short note about what worked well this week and what got in the way. These notes accumulate over time and become a powerful record of what your life actually looks like — not the idealized version, the real one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set intentions for the coming week">
    Choose up to three intentions for the week ahead. These aren't tasks — they're directional statements like *Make progress on the proposal* or *Protect mornings for deep work*. Your intentions appear at the top of your Week view as a quiet reminder of what you decided mattered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Do your weekly review on Sunday evening or Monday morning, before the week gathers speed. Sunday evening works well if you like to enter the week with clarity already in place. Monday morning works well if you prefer to reflect once the week is about to begin. Pick one and protect the time — even thirty minutes is enough.
</Tip>

## Asking for Help Planning

Sometimes you need a second perspective. You can ask the Life OS AI assistant a direct question from within the Reflect space and get a personalised suggestion based on your actual goals, habits, and schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI assistant in Reflect">
    Tap the **Ask** button at the bottom of the Reflect screen. A chat panel opens alongside your review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your question">
    Type a natural question such as:
    - *What should I focus on this week?*
    - *Which habit has been slipping the most lately?*
    - *Am I making progress on my health goal?*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the suggestion">
    The assistant reads your goals, recent habit completions, and weekly intentions to give you a grounded, specific answer — not generic advice. It might suggest deprioritising one goal because another is close to a milestone, or flag that a habit hasn't been completed in ten days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on it or dismiss it">
    The suggestion is a prompt, not a directive. Tap **Apply to this week** to carry any recommendations forward, or simply read and close the panel. You're always in charge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **AI assistance during early access.** The AI assistant is available to all users during the early access period, with a daily interaction limit to keep the experience sustainable as the system scales. The limit resets every 24 hours. You'll see a counter in the Ask panel so you always know where you stand.
</Note>

## Finding Past Entries

Your journal and weekly reviews build into a searchable archive of your growth over time.

<Accordion title="Browse by date">
  In the Reflect screen, scroll the left-hand timeline to move back through weeks and months. Tap any date to open that day's journal entry, or tap a week label to open the weekly review for that period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Search by keyword">
  Tap the search icon in the Reflect header and type any word or phrase. Life OS searches across daily journal entries and weekly review notes simultaneously. Results show the date, a preview snippet, and whether the match came from a daily entry or a weekly review.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Filter by life area or goal">
  Use the **Filter** option in the search results to narrow entries to a specific Life Area or Goal. This is useful when you want to read back through everything you've written about, say, your *Health* area or a long-running project — without scrolling through unrelated entries.
</Accordion>
