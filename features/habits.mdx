Habits in Life OS are built on a simple philosophy: a habit should feel like growth, not homework. Most habit trackers turn daily practice into a pressure game — miss a day, lose your streak, feel like a failure, give up. Life OS takes a different approach. Tracking is here to show you honest patterns over time, to celebrate consistency without punishing imperfection, and to make it easy to keep going even after a hard week. Your habits belong to you — not to a counter.

## Creating a Habit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Open **Habits** in the sidebar and tap **New Habit**. Give the habit a short, active name that describes the behaviour — not the outcome. "Read for 20 minutes", "Take a walk", and "Write morning pages" work well. Names like "Be healthier" describe a goal, not an action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the frequency">
    Choose how often the habit should occur:
    - **Daily** — appears in Today every day
    - **Specific days** — choose which days of the week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

    Start with the minimum frequency that still feels meaningful. It is easier to add days later than to recover from a punishing schedule you set in a moment of ambition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to a Life Area (optional)">
    Link the habit to a Life Area to keep your tracking organised and to see, over time, which areas of your life are getting daily attention and which are being neglected. This step is optional — unassigned habits still appear in Today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a reminder time (optional)">
    If timing matters for this habit, tap **Reminder** and choose a time. Life OS will send a notification at that time on each scheduled day. Reminders are off by default — use them for habits where context-switching is hard, not as a crutch for every item on the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking In

You can mark habits done from two places:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From Today">
    Every habit scheduled for the current day appears in the **Habits** section of your Today view. Tap the circle next to a habit to check it off. The check-in is timestamped automatically. Long-press to open the detail card if you want to add a brief note — how it felt, what you noticed, any variation you made.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the Habits page">
    Open **Habits** in the sidebar to see all your habits and today's status for each. You can check off items here as well, and you can also log a check-in for **yesterday** if you forgot to mark it in the moment. Life OS allows backdating by one day to accommodate real life without opening the door to wholesale retroactive editing.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Streaks and Patterns

Life OS shows two kinds of data about your habits:

**Streaks** count your current consecutive days of completion. They are displayed on each habit card as a simple number. Streaks are satisfying to build and useful for building momentum — but they are one signal, not a verdict.

**Weekly completion patterns** show you a rolling visual of which days you completed the habit over the past few weeks. This is more useful than a streak for understanding your actual rhythm. You might notice you always skip Fridays, or that your morning habits have a perfect record but your evening ones rarely happen. That insight is what habit tracking is for.

<Accordion title="What happens to my streak if I miss a day?">
  Your current streak resets to zero, but your history — and your overall completion rate — is unchanged. The rolling 30-day view will still show your genuine pattern accurately. Missing one day does not erase everything you built, and the 30-day view makes that plain. See the Note below for more on how Life OS approaches this.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a habit?">
  Yes. Open a habit card and tap the settings icon in the top-right corner. You can rename the habit, change its frequency, update the reminder, or delete it. Deleting a habit removes all its history permanently — if you want to stop tracking something without losing the data, tap **Archive** instead.
</Accordion>

## Habit Routines

A Routine is a named group of habits that belong together — typically anchored to a time of day or a context. Common examples include a **Morning Routine** (water, stretch, journal) or an **Wind-Down Routine** (phone away, read, gratitude list).

To create a Routine:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Routines">
    From the Habits page, tap **Routines** in the top navigation, then tap **New Routine**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and time the Routine">
    Give the Routine a name and optionally assign it a time of day. The time is used to position the Routine block in your Today view — it will appear in chronological order alongside your scheduled events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add habits to the Routine">
    Tap **Add habits** and select from your existing habits. A habit can belong to one Routine at most. Habits inside a Routine still appear as individual check-ins, but they are grouped visually under the Routine name in Today.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Routines help with batching — when several habits live under one heading, the psychological lift of starting is lower. "Do my Morning Routine" is a simpler instruction to your brain than "do five separate things."

<Note>
  Missing a day in Life OS **never resets meaningful progress**. Instead of a streak-or-nothing counter, the app tracks your completion across a rolling 30-day window. You will always see your real completion rate — say, 24 out of 30 days — rather than a streak of zero because yesterday was hard. The goal of habit tracking is self-knowledge and gentle accountability, not a perfect score.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Start with one or two habits, not ten. It is tempting to redesign your entire day the moment you open a new system. Resist it. Pick the one or two habits that would genuinely change your life if they became automatic, build those until they feel effortless, and then add the next ones. The system will be here when you are ready.
</Tip>
