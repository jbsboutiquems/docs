Today is your starting point — the one place you open each morning to understand what the day holds and decide how you want to show up in it. Rather than jumping between your calendar, task list, and journal, Today pulls the most relevant information from every part of Life OS into a single, unhurried surface. It is not a to-do list. It is a daily orientation that helps you move through your day with intention instead of reaction.

## What You See in Today

Today is designed to surface only what is relevant right now, drawing from the rest of your Life OS automatically.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Upcoming Events" icon="calendar">
    Your schedule for today, pulled directly from the Schedule view. Events are listed in chronological order so you can see the shape of your day at a glance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Habit Check-ins" icon="check-circle">
    Every habit scheduled for today appears here as a simple check-in. One tap marks it done — no navigation required.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Goal Next Steps" icon="flag">
    The immediate next action from each of your active goals surfaces here, so forward momentum is always one step away.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Journal Prompt" icon="pencil">
    A short, rotating reflection prompt sits at the bottom of Today to invite a moment of pause before or after your day.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Checking Off Items

You can act on almost everything in Today without leaving the view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark a habit done">
    Tap the circle next to any habit to check it off. The habit turns from outlined to filled and your streak updates instantly. If you want to add a quick note to the check-in — like how the run felt — long-press the habit to open the detail card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete a goal next step">
    Tap the checkbox beside any goal next step to mark it complete. Life OS automatically surfaces the following next step the next time Today loads, keeping your goal moving without requiring you to open the Goals section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge an event">
    Events from your Schedule are read-only in Today — they are there for awareness. Tap any event to open the full detail in Schedule if you need to reschedule or add notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Daily Intention

At the very top of Today sits a single text field labelled **Your intention for today**. This is an optional one-sentence statement — a personal anchor for the day. It could be a focus ("Finish the draft and rest"), a feeling ("Be fully present with family"), or a reminder to yourself ("Do less, but better").

To set your intention, tap the field at the top of Today and type your sentence. It persists throughout the day and becomes part of your daily record, which you can review later in the Reflect section. Setting an intention takes under ten seconds and has an outsized effect on how deliberately you move through your day.

## Navigating from Today

Today is a hub, not a destination. Every item you see links directly to its source.

| Item in Today | Where it links |
|---|---|
| An event | Schedule — day view for that event |
| A habit check-in | Habits — full history and settings for that habit |
| A goal next step | Goals — the full goal card with all steps |
| The journal prompt | Reflect — opens a new daily journal entry |
| Your intention | Reflect — saved to your daily record automatically |

Use these links when you need more context or want to make changes. For a quick, flowing morning, you can do everything you need without leaving Today.

<Tip>
  Open Today before you open your email or messages. Spending even two minutes with your intention and your habits before the world makes demands of you changes the quality of the entire day. Life OS is designed to be your first tab, not your fifth.
</Tip>

<Note>
  **Calm Mode** is a toggle in the top-right corner of Today. When enabled, it hides everything except your three most important items for the day — one event, one habit, and one goal next step. Use Calm Mode on high-pressure days, or whenever the full view feels like too much. Your data is untouched; Calm Mode only changes what is displayed.
</Note>
