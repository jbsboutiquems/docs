Your schedule is more than a list of appointments — it's a map of how you're choosing to spend your life. The Schedule feature in Life OS gives you four distinct views so you can zoom in on today, step back to see the week, or look ahead at the bigger picture. Whether you're protecting deep work time or making sure you haven't overloaded a Tuesday, Schedule keeps your commitments visible and honest.

## Views Overview

Each view is designed for a different planning horizon. Switch between them freely — they all stay in sync.

  <Card title="Week View" icon="calendar-week">

## Adding Events

Creating an event takes just a few taps. Open any view and tap an empty time slot to start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap a time slot">
    In Day or Week view, tap directly on the time you want the event to start. A new event card opens at that time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a title and duration">
    Give the event a clear name and drag the bottom edge to set how long it runs — or type a specific end time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag a life area">
    Assign the event to one of your Life Areas (for example, *Work*, *Health*, or *Family*). The event inherits that area's colour, so your schedule stays visually organised.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional details">
    Include a location, notes, or a link. These show up in the event detail view when you tap it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Done** and the event lands on your schedule immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Focus Blocks

A Focus Block is a protected slot of time you reserve for deep work, a creative project, or an important habit routine. Unlike a regular event, a Focus Block signals to you — and to your day — that this time is already spoken for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Focus Block">
    Tap an empty time slot and choose **Focus Block** instead of Event. Give it a name that describes the work, such as *Deep Work: Report Draft* or *Morning Run*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set duration and recurrence">
    Choose how long the block runs. If this is a daily practice, toggle **Repeat** and select the days it should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a life area or project">
    Tag the block to a Life Area or Project so it rolls up correctly in your progress views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See it on Today">
    Focus Blocks automatically appear in your **Today** view under *Scheduled Focus*, giving you a clear anchor for the day's most important work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Schedule at least one "open space" block each day — an unmarked slot you deliberately leave empty. It absorbs overruns, gives you room to think, and stops your day from becoming a wall-to-wall gauntlet.
</Tip>

## Connecting to Goals and Habits

Your schedule becomes most powerful when it's wired to the rest of your Life OS. Focus Blocks can be linked directly to what you're trying to accomplish.

<Accordion title="Link a Focus Block to a Goal's next step">
  When you create or edit a Focus Block, tap **Link to Goal** and choose the Goal you're working on. Then select the specific next step you plan to tackle during that block. When you mark the block complete, Life OS prompts you to update the step's progress — so your Goals tracker stays current without any extra admin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Link a Focus Block to a Habit routine">
  Open the Focus Block and tap **Link to Habit**. Choose the habit you want this block to support. Completing the block counts as a check-in for that habit, keeping your streak intact and your routine tied to real scheduled time rather than a floating reminder.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="See connections at a glance">
  In Day view, any block linked to a Goal or Habit shows a small icon beside its title — a flag for Goals and a ring for Habits. Tap the block to see the full context: which goal it feeds, which habit it supports, and what progress looks like so far.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  **Calendar sync is coming.** Google Calendar and Apple Calendar integration is on the roadmap. When it arrives, events from your external calendars will appear directly in your Schedule views alongside your Life OS blocks, so everything lives in one place.
</Note>
