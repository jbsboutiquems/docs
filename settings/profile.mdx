Your profile is the foundation of your Life OS experience. Keeping it up to date ensures that reminders fire at the right time, your name appears the way you want it, and your account stays secure. Everything from your display name to your password lives in one place, so you never have to hunt for settings.

## Accessing Your Profile

To open your profile and account settings, tap your **avatar** or **initials** in the top-right corner of any screen, then select **Profile & Settings** from the dropdown menu. This is your central hub for all personal and account-level preferences.

## Display Name and Avatar

Your display name appears throughout Life OS — in greetings, review prompts, and shared spaces. To update it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile & Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials in the top-right corner and select **Profile & Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name">
    Click the pencil icon next to your current name, type your preferred name, and press **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your avatar">
    Click your current avatar image to open the avatar editor. You can **upload a photo** from your device or choose from the built-in illustration set. Crop and confirm your selection, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your avatar doesn't have to be a photo — pick an illustration that feels right for where you are right now. You can always change it.
</Tip>

## Timezone

Life OS uses your timezone to schedule every event, habit reminder, and daily prompt at the correct local time. If your timezone is wrong, reminders will fire at the wrong hour and your Today view may show the wrong date.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile & Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials and select **Profile & Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Timezone field">
    Scroll to the **Account Preferences** section and find the **Timezone** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your timezone">
    Search for or scroll to your city or UTC offset and select it. Your preference is saved automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you travel frequently, update your timezone when you arrive in a new location so that habit reminders and review prompts stay aligned with your day.
</Note>

## Account Email

Your account email is used for sign-in, password resets, and important account notices. To update it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile & Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials and select **Profile & Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your email address">
    In the **Account** section, click **Change Email**. Enter your new email address and click **Send Confirmation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    A confirmation link is sent to the **new** address. Open that email and click the link to complete the change. Your old address remains active until you confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile & Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials and select **Profile & Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Security">
    Scroll to the **Security** section and click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Click **Update Password** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Prefer not to manage a password at all? Switch to **magic-link sign-in** in the Security section. Each time you log in, Life OS emails you a one-click link — no password required.
</Tip>

## Deleting Your Account

If you decide to leave Life OS, you can request account deletion directly from your profile page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile & Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials and select **Profile & Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Danger Zone">
    Scroll to the bottom of the page to the **Danger Zone** section and click **Delete My Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Read the confirmation prompt carefully, type the required phrase, and click **Permanently Delete Account** to submit your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All of your data — goals, habits, journal entries, schedule events, and reviews — is permanently removed within **30 days** of your deletion request. You will receive an email confirming when the deletion is complete.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is irreversible. Once confirmed, your data cannot be recovered. If you think you might return to Life OS, consider exporting your data first — see [Data & Privacy](/settings/data-privacy) for instructions.
</Warning>
