The right reminder at the right moment can be the difference between a habit that sticks and one that fades. Life OS puts you in control of every notification it sends — you choose what fires, when it fires, and when you need silence. The goal is gentle, well-timed prompts that support your day rather than interrupt it.

## Notification Types

Life OS sends several distinct types of notifications, each with its own purpose and timing controls.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Habit Reminders" icon="repeat">
    Each habit can have its own reminder time. Set a nudge for just the habits that need a little push — not every habit requires one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily Intention Prompt" icon="sun">
    A morning prompt that invites you to set your focus for the day. Fires at a custom time you choose — typically just before your day begins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily Review Prompt" icon="moon">
    An evening prompt that opens your end-of-day check-in. Fires at a custom time, ideally when you're winding down and ready to reflect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Weekly Review Reminder" icon="calendar">
    A single weekly reminder on the day and time you choose, prompting you to close out the week, review progress, and plan ahead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Goal Nudges" icon="flag">
    A gentle alert when a goal has had no recorded activity for a number of days you define. Helps you notice when something has drifted off your radar.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting Up Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials in the top-right corner, select **Profile & Settings**, then navigate to the **Notifications** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant browser or app permission">
    If you haven't already, click **Enable Notifications** and approve the browser or device permission prompt. Life OS cannot send notifications without this permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each notification type">
    Toggle each notification type on or off individually. For each active type, set your preferred delivery time using the time picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set habit-level reminders">
    For habit reminders, head to **Habits** in the main navigation, open an individual habit, and set a reminder time directly on that habit. This lets you give each habit its own schedule rather than a single blanket reminder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure goal nudge sensitivity">
    For goal nudges, set the inactivity threshold — for example, **3 days** or **7 days** — to control how long a goal can go untouched before you're prompted to revisit it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quiet Hours

Quiet Hours let you define a window of time during which Life OS holds all notifications, no matter what they are. This is perfect for protecting your sleep, deep-work blocks, or any time you don't want to be interrupted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications settings">
    Go to **Profile & Settings > Notifications** and scroll to the **Quiet Hours** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Quiet Hours">
    Toggle **Quiet Hours** on. Set your **start time** (for example, 10:00 pm) and your **end time** (for example, 7:00 am).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your window">
    Click **Save**. Any notification that would have fired during this window is silently held and not delivered retroactively — it simply won't appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're new to Life OS notifications, start with just two: the **Daily Intention** prompt in the morning and the **Daily Review** prompt in the evening. Once those feel natural, layer in habit reminders only for the specific habits where a nudge genuinely helps. More notifications rarely means more progress — the right ones do.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Notification delivery depends on your browser or device granting permission to Life OS. If you granted permission previously and notifications have stopped arriving, check your browser or device notification settings to ensure Life OS hasn't been silenced at the system level.
</Note>
