Your life data belongs to you — not to advertisers, not to algorithms, and not to anyone else. Life OS is built on a simple commitment: everything you write, plan, and reflect on stays private, is never sold, and is yours to take with you at any time. This page explains exactly what is stored, how it is protected, and how to export or delete it whenever you choose.

## What Data Is Stored

Life OS stores only what you put into it. Here is a complete picture of the information held on your account:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Account information">
    Your email address and display name. No payment details are stored directly — payments are handled by our third-party payment processor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Life areas, goals, and projects">
    The life areas you've defined, every goal you've captured, the steps and progress records attached to those goals, and any projects you've created under your life areas.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Habits and routines">
    Your habit definitions, their schedules, and the full completion history for every habit you've tracked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Schedule events">
    All events you've added to your day, week, or month views, including their dates, times, and any notes attached.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Journal entries and reflections">
    Everything you've written in the daily review, weekly review, and any free-form journal entries — stored as private, encrypted text.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Life OS does not pull health data, location data, or information from external services without an explicit connection that you initiate and can revoke at any time. External integrations are opt-in only.
</Note>

## Data Storage

All of your data is stored on secure servers and **encrypted at rest**. Connections between your device and Life OS use TLS encryption in transit. Access to stored data is strictly limited to the systems that serve it back to you.

<Note>
  **Offline access and device-level storage** are on the Life OS roadmap. When available, this will let you access and edit your data without an internet connection, with changes synced when you reconnect. We'll announce this feature in the changelog when it launches.
</Note>

## AI Features and Your Data

Some features in Life OS use AI assistance — for example, goal-framing suggestions, reflection prompts, and weekly review summaries. Here is exactly how your data is handled in those contexts:

- Your data is used **only to generate a response for you** in the current session.
- It is **not used to train AI models** — by Life OS or by any AI provider we work with.
- It is **not shared with third parties** for any purpose other than generating your in-app response.
- AI-generated content is never stored separately or linked to an identity outside of your own account.

You can opt out of AI features entirely in **Profile & Settings > AI Preferences** without affecting any other part of Life OS.

## Exporting Your Data

You can request a full export of everything in your Life OS account at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data & Privacy settings">
    Tap your avatar or initials in the top-right corner, select **Profile & Settings**, then navigate to the **Data & Privacy** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request your export">
    Click **Export My Data**. Life OS will begin preparing a complete export of your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your file">
    Within **24 hours**, you'll receive an email with a secure download link. The export is delivered as a structured **JSON file** containing all of your account data — goals, habits, journal entries, schedule events, reviews, and account information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Export your data before deleting your account. Once deletion is confirmed, your data cannot be recovered, and the export window closes. Take a few minutes to download and save your export somewhere safe first.
</Tip>

## Deleting Your Data

You have two options depending on what you want to do:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Reset your data only">
    Resetting removes all of your content — life areas, goals, habits, journal entries, events, and reviews — while keeping your account active. Use this if you want a clean start without closing your account.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Data & Privacy settings">
        Go to **Profile & Settings > Data & Privacy** and scroll to the **Reset** section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Initiate the reset">
        Click **Reset All Data**, read the confirmation prompt carefully, and type the required phrase to confirm.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Click **Reset My Data**. All content is permanently deleted immediately. Your account and login credentials remain intact.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Delete your account entirely">
    Deleting your account removes all data **and** closes your account permanently. You will no longer be able to sign in.

    Full instructions are on the [Profile](/settings/profile) settings page. All data is permanently removed within **30 days** of your deletion request.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Life OS complies with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and other applicable privacy regulations. If you are a resident of a jurisdiction with specific data rights — including the right to access, rectify, or erase your data — you can exercise those rights at any time through the Data & Privacy settings page, or by contacting our support team directly.
</Note>
