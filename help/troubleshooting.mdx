Most issues in Life OS have a quick fix. Work through the steps below for the problem you're seeing — each one covers the most likely causes first so you can get back on track without losing time. If something isn't listed here, the [Contact](/help/contact) page explains how to reach the team directly.

<Accordion title="I can't log in">
  Start with the basics before anything else:

  1. **Check your email address** — make sure there are no typos and that you're using the address you signed up with.
  2. **Reset your password** — click **Forgot Password** on the login screen. If you use magic-link login, request a new link instead.
  3. **Check your spam or junk folder** — password reset and magic-link emails occasionally get filtered. Add `support@offscript.life` to your contacts to prevent this.
  4. **Try a different browser** — browser extensions (especially ad-blockers or privacy tools) can interfere with the login flow. Try an incognito window or a different browser to rule this out.

  If none of these work, contact support with your account email and the exact error message you're seeing.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My habits are not showing in Today">
  If a habit you expect to see isn't appearing in the Today view, check the following:

  1. **Frequency settings** — open the habit and confirm that today's day of the week is included in the frequency. For example, a habit set to weekdays only won't appear on Saturday or Sunday.
  2. **Paused habits** — habits can be paused temporarily. Check whether the habit has a **Paused** badge and resume it if needed.
  3. **Archived habits** — archived habits are hidden from all views. Go to **Habits → Archived** to find and restore it.

  Once you've confirmed the frequency and status, the habit should appear in Today for the correct days.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications are not arriving">
  Notification delivery depends on a few layers working together:

  1. **Browser permissions** — open your browser's site settings for the Life OS domain and confirm that notifications are set to **Allow**. If they're blocked, re-enable them and reload the app.
  2. **Quiet Hours** — check **Settings → Notifications → Quiet Hours**. If Quiet Hours are active during the time you expect notifications, they'll be silenced until the window ends.
  3. **Notification type toggles** — individual notification types (habit reminders, daily review prompts, etc.) can each be turned on or off. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and confirm the specific type you want is enabled.

  On mobile browsers, you may also need to check your device's system-level notification settings for the browser app itself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My schedule events disappeared">
  Before assuming anything is lost, run through these checks:

  1. **Date range** — confirm you're looking at the correct week or month. It's easy to navigate away without noticing.
  2. **Life Area filter** — if a Life Area filter is active in the Schedule view, events from other areas will be hidden. Clear the filter to show all events.
  3. **Refresh the page** — a standard page refresh (F5 or Cmd+R) will re-fetch your data from the server and often resolves display inconsistencies.

  If events are genuinely missing after these checks, contact support with the approximate dates of the missing events.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The app feels slow or unresponsive">
  Performance issues are usually local rather than a server problem. Try these steps in order:

  1. **Hard refresh** — press **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows/Linux or **Cmd+Shift+R** on Mac to bypass the cache and reload fresh assets.
  2. **Clear browser cache** — go to your browser's settings and clear cached files and cookies for the Life OS domain.
  3. **Check your connection** — run a quick speed test or try loading another site to rule out a network issue.
  4. **Disable extensions** — try an incognito window with extensions disabled to see if a browser extension is causing the slowdown.

  If the app remains slow after all of the above, it may be a temporary server issue. Check the Life OS status page or contact support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I accidentally deleted a goal or habit">
  Deleted items cannot currently be recovered — once something is deleted, it's gone. An **undo** feature is on the roadmap and will be added in a future update.

  In the meantime, you can recreate the goal or habit from scratch. If you remember the details, it only takes a minute to get back to where you were.

  <Warning>
    If the deletion just happened and you believe your data may still be recoverable, contact support as soon as possible at support@offscript.life. Include your account email and the name of the deleted item — the team will do everything they can to help.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Note>
  If none of the steps above resolve your issue, the Life OS team is ready to help. Head to the [Contact](/help/contact) page to get in touch directly.
</Note>
