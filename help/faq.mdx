Life OS is built to be picked up and used on day one — no lengthy configuration required. If you're wondering how something works, whether a feature exists, or what happens to your data, you'll find the answers here. These are the questions the Life OS community asks most often.

## Getting Started

<Accordion title="Is Life OS free?">
  Yes — Life OS is completely free during early access. No credit card is required to sign up, and you get full access to all current features from the moment you create your account. Paid tiers with advanced features are planned for the future, but billing is not live yet. You'll always get fair notice before anything changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What devices and browsers does Life OS support?">
  Life OS is a web app that runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge are all fully supported. You can use it on desktop, laptop, or tablet today. A dedicated mobile app is on the roadmap, so keep an eye on the changelog for updates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to configure everything before it's useful?">
  Not at all. A first useful day beats a perfect setup every time. The best way to start is small: add one life area that matters to you right now, one goal under it, and one habit you want to build. The system is designed to grow with you — you can layer in more structure whenever it feels right, not before.
</Accordion>

## Features

<Accordion title="What's the difference between a Goal and a Project?">
  Goals are long-horizon directions — the kind of outcome you work toward over months or years, like *Get stronger* or *Build a creative practice*. Projects are time-bound efforts with a clear done state, like *Run a 5k in under 30 minutes* or *Launch the podcast*. Projects live under life areas and can be linked to goals, so your day-to-day work always connects back to what matters most.

  Learn more on the [Projects](/system/projects) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I connect Life OS to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar?">
  Calendar integration is on the roadmap. For now, you manage your schedule entirely within Life OS using the day, week, and month views. Import from external calendars is coming — when it arrives, you'll be able to sync existing commitments without re-entering anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does the AI assistance work?">
  The AI in Life OS draws on your goals, habits, and journal entries to offer personalised suggestions — things like helping you plan the week ahead, spotting patterns in your reflections, or nudging you toward a neglected priority. Your data is **never** used to train AI models. During early access, AI interactions have daily usage limits, which will be communicated clearly inside the app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the Daily Review?">
  The Daily Review is a short end-of-day check-in designed to take around five minutes. It helps you close open loops from the day, note one win worth remembering, and set your single top priority for tomorrow. Doing it consistently is one of the highest-leverage habits you can build inside Life OS.

  See the full walkthrough on the [Daily Review](/system/daily-review) page.
</Accordion>

## Account & Data

<Accordion title="How is my data stored and protected?">
  All your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Life OS never sells your data to third parties, and it is never used to train AI models or shared with advertisers. For the full picture — including data retention policies and your rights — visit the [Data & Privacy](/settings/data-privacy) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
  Yes. Go to **Settings → Data & Privacy → Export My Data** and submit your request. Your full data export will be delivered as a JSON file within 24 hours. Everything you've entered — goals, habits, journal entries, schedule events — is included.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
  Go to your **Profile settings** and scroll down to the **Danger Zone** section. Confirming account deletion permanently removes all your data. The deletion is processed within 30 days in line with our data retention policy. This action cannot be undone, so make sure you've exported anything you want to keep first.
</Accordion>
