## Email Support

For anything that needs a human response, email the team at:

**support@offscript.life**

To help the team resolve your issue as quickly as possible, include the following in your message:

<Accordion title="What to include in a support email">
  - **Your account email** — the address you use to log in to Life OS
  - **A description of the issue** — what you expected to happen and what actually happened
  - **Steps to reproduce** — a numbered list of what you did leading up to the problem, if applicable
  - **Any error messages** — copy the exact text or attach a screenshot
  - **Browser and device** — for example, *Chrome 124 on macOS Sonoma* or *Safari on iPad*

  The more detail you provide upfront, the less back-and-forth is needed — and the faster you'll get a resolution.
</Accordion>

## Response Times

During early access, the team aims to respond to all support emails within **1–2 business days**. Most questions get a reply sooner than that. Complex issues involving data investigation or bug reproduction may take a little longer — if that's the case, you'll receive an acknowledgement with an estimated timeline.

## Sending Feedback

Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next. Feature requests, workflow suggestions, and general impressions all go into the roadmap process — nothing is filed away and forgotten.

You can send feedback in two ways:

- **Email** — write to support@offscript.life with your idea or suggestion. No specific format needed; just tell the team what you're thinking.
- **In-app feedback button** — go to **Settings → Send Feedback** to submit directly from inside Life OS. This is the fastest path and automatically attaches helpful context like your current app version.

Don't hold back on the small stuff either. The best product improvements often come from an offhand comment about something that felt slightly off.

## Reporting Bugs

If you've found something that looks broken, the team wants to know about it as soon as possible.

**Steps to report a bug:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the in-app reporter">
    Go to **Settings → Send Feedback → Bug Report**. This is the preferred method — it automatically captures your app version, browser environment, and recent activity logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what happened">
    Write a brief description of the bug: what you were doing, what you expected, and what went wrong. Numbered steps to reproduce are especially helpful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a screenshot">
    If you can capture the problem visually, attach a screenshot. A picture of an error message or broken layout saves a lot of back-and-forth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit or email">
    Submit via the in-app form, or send the same details to **support@offscript.life** if you'd prefer email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
