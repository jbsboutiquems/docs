## How Life OS Works

Life OS is built around a simple loop: define what matters, show up daily, and review regularly to keep improving. Every feature is designed to serve that loop — nothing more, nothing less.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your life areas and goals">
    Start by naming the areas of your life that deserve attention — things like Health, Career, Relationships, or Creative Work. Then add at least one goal inside each area to give yourself a direction to move in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build daily habits and your schedule">
    Capture the small, consistent actions that compound over time as habits. Block out your commitments in the schedule so you can see your week at a glance and protect the time that matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Today as your command surface">
    Every morning, open the Today view. It surfaces your goal next steps, today's habits, and your scheduled commitments in one calm place — so you always know exactly what to focus on right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reflect weekly to improve">
    At the end of each week, open Reflect. Review what you did, notice what's working, and adjust your goals and habits for the week ahead. This is where the system gets smarter over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
