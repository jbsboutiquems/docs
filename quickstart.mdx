You don't need a perfect system before you start. This guide walks you through the fastest path to a working Life OS: create your account, lay down a few building blocks, and open Today to see it all come together. The whole thing takes less than ten minutes.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A **modern web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge — all work great)
- An **email address** to create your account

No app download required. Life OS runs entirely in your browser.

## Set up in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [app.offscript.life](https://app.offscript.life) and click **Get Started**. Enter your email address and choose a password, then check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the verification link. Once confirmed, you'll land on your Life OS dashboard for the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your first Life Area">
    Life areas are the top-level categories that organise everything in your system — think of them as the chapters of your life. Common examples include **Health**, **Career**, **Relationships**, **Finances**, and **Creative Work**, but yours should reflect what actually matters to you.

    On the welcome screen (or from the sidebar under **Life Areas**), click **New Life Area**, give it a name, and pick a colour to make it easy to spot. You can always add more later — start with just one or two.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your first Goal">
    Inside your new life area, click **New Goal**. Give it a clear name that describes the outcome you want — for example, *Run a 5K by June* or *Read 12 books this year*.

    Next, add at least one **next step**: the smallest concrete action that moves you toward that goal. This is what will show up in your Today view, so keep it specific and completable in a single session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Habit">
    From the sidebar, open **Habits** and click **New Habit**. Enter a short description of a daily action you want to build — something small and sustainable, like *10 minutes of movement* or *Write 3 sentences in my journal*.

    Attach it to a life area if you like, then save. Life OS will prompt you to check it off each day in Today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Today">
    Click **Today** in the sidebar. You'll see your goal's next step and your new habit waiting for you. Work through them, check each one off, and you've just completed your first intentional day in Life OS.

    That's the whole loop — show up, do the work, check it off. Everything else in Life OS is built to support that rhythm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Resist the urge to set up every life area, goal, and habit on day one. An over-configured system is easy to abandon. Start with one area, one goal, and one habit — get a feel for the loop first, then expand at your own pace.
</Tip>
